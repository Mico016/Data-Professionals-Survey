<h2> Overview </h2>
This report analyzes the satisfaction levels of employees working in data-related roles, using factors such as salary, work environment, job position, and other demographic attributes. The report shows different insights from the data gathered from numerous respondents of employees on a data related field highlighting patterns across age groups, countries, compensation ranges, and overall sentiment. The dashboard is designed with a clean, easy-to-read layout and modern visual elements, presenting the data through intuitive charts and graphs. This makes it simple to explore trends, compare groups, and understand the key drivers of employee satisfaction within the data profession.
